# Activities and Games

Optional activities for Microsoft Build Talks that can encourage participation between sessions. All activities and prizes below are proposals, not confirmed event arrangements. Participation should always be voluntary.

## Between-session icebreakers

### 1. Tech knowledge quiz

Ask quick questions about Microsoft, AI, and cybersecurity. Keep the questions short and accessible to attendees with different levels of experience.

### 2. Guess the term

Read a definition and invite attendees to raise a hand and name the matching term. Award points if a scoring system has been agreed in advance.

### 3. Who is the tech profile?

Give a few clues about a well-known technology figure and let the audience guess who it is. Use accurate, respectful clues.

## Longer interactive activities

### 4. Personal branding challenge

Invite volunteers to introduce themselves in 30 seconds as if they were meeting an employer. Let the audience vote for a favorite only if participants are comfortable with that format.

### 5. Cybersecurity scenario

Show a prepared, fictional phishing message and ask, “What would you do?” Discuss the clues in the message and safe next steps. Do not use a real participant's account, credentials, or personal information.

### 6. Goal wall

Invite attendees to write a skill they want to learn on a sticky note and add it to a shared board. Ask before photographing notes, especially if they include names or other personal information.

## Prizes and logistics

- Confirm any official gifts or prizes with the organizers before advertising them. If none are confirmed, run the activities without material prizes or use another option approved by the team.
- Assign an activity lead and a 5–10 minute slot in the agenda for each game.
- Prepare and review questions and scenarios at least two days before the event.
- Make every activity opt-in. Never pressure anyone to go on stage or participate.
