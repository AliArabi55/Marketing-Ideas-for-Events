# Before the Event

16 promotional video concepts for **Microsoft Build Talks at ITI, Suez Canal University**. Use `[DATE]`, `[TIME]`, `[VENUE]`, and `[NUMBER OF DAYS]` as placeholders until the organizers confirm the details.

> Any notebooks, refreshments, gifts, or prizes mentioned below are proposed scene details, not confirmed arrangements. Do not advertise them unless they are officially approved. Staged scenes must be agreed upon by everyone involved; never turn them into real pranks on unsuspecting people.

## Video ideas

### 1. Guarding the promotional notebook

- **Concept:** A teammate reaches for a branded notebook. The person holding it protects it with exaggerated, playful seriousness, as if guarding treasure: “Not yet... this is for the event!” Make it clearly staged and friendly.
- **Message:** The team is getting ready for Microsoft Build Talks on `[DATE]` at `[VENUE]`.
- **Note:** The notebook is a prop suggestion, not a promise that attendees will receive one.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcgWVguIFjc/?dmpr=eDc5ZDIyNDVuMmhs)

### 2. The team leader rallies everyone

- **Concept:** A team leader gathers the group: “Let’s fill the room on `[DATE]`!” Quick cuts show each teammate inviting people in their own way: in a hallway, in a group chat, or near the campus cafe.
- **Message:** The whole team is inviting you to Microsoft Build Talks at ITI, Suez Canal University.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DejRMZHSD86/?evlk=MTA5Y2h1M20xaHc0aw==)

### 3. A passerby gets the full experience

- **Concept:** A passerby is invited in and arrives unsure. A sequence follows their day: attending talks, looking up to find that time has flown by, joining an optional activity, and leaving happy: “I was only passing by!” Adjust every scene to match the confirmed agenda and arrangements.
- **Message:** Come for a full day of talks and activities.
- **Note:** Refreshments and notebook prizes are only possible scene details, not confirmed offers.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbsAk00hCl1/?jplx=dGN2YzNpenFjbmF2)

### 4. The dropped bag (safe adaptation)

- **Concept:** A teammate's bag tips over, and other teammates help collect the items. Event flyers and stickers are among them, prompting a reveal: “These are the preparations for Microsoft Build Talks on `[DATE]`!”
- **Note:** Stage the moment with consenting participants. Do not involve or film unsuspecting passersby. This is an adaptation; it has not been verified shot-by-shot against the reference.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfFOB6lTtp8/?avpt=MTJwY2FubXZtdHU5ZQ==)

### 5. The four questions ⭐

- **Concept:** Ask three light introductory questions on camera, then make the fourth question the reveal: “Want to learn about AI, cybersecurity, project management, and personal branding? Join Microsoft Build Talks at ITI, Suez Canal University, on `[DATE]` at `[TIME]`.”
- **Message:** Four skill areas in one event: AI, cybersecurity, project management, and personal branding.
- **Origin:** Original idea from the event organizer; no reference link.

### 6. A few-days-left reminder

- **Concept:** A short, direct reminder posted `[NUMBER OF DAYS]` days before the event. Pair the countdown with quick preparation clips: “Only `[NUMBER OF DAYS]` days until Microsoft Build Talks. Register now!”
- **Note:** The reference video could not be viewed because of Instagram age restrictions. This written execution is a new suggestion, not a description of the original.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dfn17CPuysb/?jplx=MWJ2eDhueXk2bmNqdg==)

### 7. A 50-year flashback ⭐

- **Concept:** In a period scene, a grandfather as a young man had the chance to buy land in Maadi for a small amount, but dismissed the opportunity to attend an Umm Kulthum concert. Cut to the present: a student dismisses an invitation to a learning event with, “No, I have something more important.”
- **Suggested ending:** “The land he missed was once inexpensive. The skill you miss today could cost you your future. Invest in yourself at Microsoft Build Talks on `[DATE]`.” Keep the comparison light and avoid presenting the story as a real historical account.
- **Origin:** Original idea from the event organizer; no reference link.

### 8. Save the Date poster

- **Concept:** A clean poster or short reveal video that announces only the confirmed date: “Save the Date — `[DATE]`.” Keep the event identity prominent and leave the remaining details for a later announcement.
- **Message:** Mark the date; more information is coming.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/De1920iMkY0/?hvrq=MTR4bjBqczgzYXV2aw==)

### 9. The dramatic entrance

- **Concept:** A teammate makes a playful, attention-grabbing entrance through a door. Everyone looks over; the entrant calmly holds up the event flyer: “Microsoft Build Talks, `[DATE]`. Register now.”
- **Note:** Agree on the movement and staging in advance; keep the physical comedy safe. This is a safe adaptation, not a verified shot-by-shot recreation.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYxJsbGOAnV/?dmpr=MXNzZHg0YXdsZ2hzMg==)

### 10. The agenda, with a runner in the background

- **Concept:** A presenter explains the agenda with complete seriousness while a teammate repeatedly runs across the background in increasingly odd but safe ways. The humor comes from the contrast; the presenter never acknowledges it.
- **Message:** Get the event agenda in about a minute. Publish only confirmed session details.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbklneFD_hk/?gwpx=aWgwZ29nZHhxbWdj)

### 11. The notebook mix-up (safe adaptation)

- **Concept:** Two teammates have similar-looking notebooks and jokingly argue, “That’s mine!” As they compare them, event details printed on the notebooks reveal Microsoft Build Talks on `[DATE]`.
- **Note:** Notebooks are set dressing, not an attendee giveaway. Stage the mix-up with consenting participants. This is an adaptation, not a verified shot-by-shot recreation.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZqONQTIphS/?hvrq=a3YwMjVkaXgzeWQ1)

### 12. The camera that is filming

- **Concept:** A teammate notices a camera, walks over curiously, looks into the lens, then holds up the event poster as if revealing a secret: “We’re getting ready for something big... Microsoft Build Talks on `[DATE]`.”
- **Message:** Reveal the event with a simple cinematic moment.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdVEKSXMdzO/?knvt=MTExYnR0eG9oaHpkbg==)

### 13. The melodrama and the mystery box

- **Concept:** Play a scene like an overly dramatic TV serial: sad music, exaggerated expressions, and an emotional conversation. At the dramatic peak, someone opens a mysterious box and finds the event invitation. Cut the music and switch to a cheerful reveal: “Microsoft Build Talks — `[DATE]`.”
- **Message:** Break the dramatic tension with a comic reveal.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcUJZ76B1pE/?fqnt=NXVjam4wZWFsbW9u)

### 14. The hallway trend ⭐

- **Concept:** Re-create the selected hallway trend with a distinctive pose or repeated movement timed to the audio. In the final shot, participants reveal the event flyer.
- **Message:** Connect a recognizable trend to the event while keeping the execution appropriate for the campus.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd1WYP6RMEy/?cnvx=MXhmYWtsZ2lldTRkeQ==)

### 15. The mock press interview (safe adaptation)

- **Concept:** One teammate holds a microphone and interviews another with ordinary questions. The interviewee dodges the important question in funny ways until finally saying: “Microsoft Build Talks, `[DATE]` at `[TIME]`, `[VENUE]`!”
- **Note:** Make it an obviously staged interview between consenting teammates, not a fake interview with an unsuspecting person. This is an adaptation; the original has not been verified shot-by-shot.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd5IV2BBKKQ/?jplx=MThqdnBjdWs1cWZwZQ==)

### 16. Cut up the poster, then reveal the full version

- **Concept:** A teammate in glasses cuts up an oversized printout while colleagues react with mock concern. The reveal: the cutout was intentional, and the complete event poster appears with confirmed registration details, including date, time, venue, and registration link or QR code.
- **Message:** Publish the complete event and registration details in one reveal.
- **Reference:** [Instagram Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcL_8r7t1zM/?jplx=aWxzYzR2MWx6dXds)
