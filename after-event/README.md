# After the Event

Post-event content can thank the community, preserve highlights, and extend the event's reach. Use verified event information and publish only media cleared for sharing.

## Content ideas

### 1. Event recap video

- **Goal:** Bring the strongest moments together in a concise highlight video.
- **Execution:** Aim for about one minute. Include the opening, a short excerpt from each talk, approved activity footage, and a group photo. Add verified event figures, such as attendee and session counts, only after the organizers confirm them.

### 2. Thank-you video

- **Goal:** Recognize attendees, speakers, sponsors, and organizers.
- **Execution:** Create a short, warm message with approved event footage. Confirm sponsor names and branding before including them.

### 3. Attendee testimonials

- **Goal:** Share what participants found useful in their own words.
- **Execution:** Collect 3–5 short responses to a prompt such as, “What is the most important thing you are taking away from today?” Get permission to record and publish each response.

### 4. Behind the scenes

- **Goal:** Show the preparation and teamwork that attendees may not have seen.
- **Execution:** Select light, respectful moments from setup and the team’s work. Avoid posting private conversations, unflattering footage, or anything a participant has not approved.

### 5. Photo album

- **Goal:** Give attendees and partners a shareable record of the day.
- **Execution:** Curate a set of strong final photos, check consent and image quality, and credit the photographer where appropriate.

## Suggested publishing sequence

1. **Event evening:** A quick thank-you story.
2. **Following day:** The event recap video.
3. **During the week:** Share testimonials and behind-the-scenes clips in separate posts.
