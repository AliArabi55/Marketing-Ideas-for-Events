# Event Marketing Ideas

A practical collection of event promotion, live coverage, post-event content, and audience activities. The detailed examples currently focus on **Microsoft Build Talks at ITI, Suez Canal University** and can be adapted for other events.

## Browse the ideas

- [Before the event](before-event/README.md): 16 promotional video concepts, including scripts-in-brief, reference links, and a publishing tracker.
- [During the event](during-event/README.md): a coverage plan for sessions, audience reactions, stories, and team responsibilities.
- [After the event](after-event/README.md): recap, thank-you, testimonial, behind-the-scenes, and photo-album ideas.
- [Activities and games](activities-and-games/README.md): optional icebreakers, interactive activities, and prize considerations.

## Event details and assumptions

- The event date, start time, and venue have not been confirmed. Replace `[DATE]`, `[TIME]`, and `[VENUE]` only after official confirmation.
- Mentions of notebooks, refreshments, gifts, or prizes are creative scene suggestions, not promises or confirmed event arrangements. Confirm them with the organizers before publishing or planning around them.
- Staged scenes should be agreed upon by everyone involved. Do not surprise, mislead, or film attendees without their consent.
- Reference links are included as inspiration. Some descriptions are adaptations and have not been verified shot-by-shot against the original videos.

## Adding an idea

Add it to the most relevant section and include enough detail for the team to evaluate and produce it:

```markdown
### Idea name

- **Goal:** What should this idea achieve?
- **Execution:** What happens, and what is needed to produce it?
- **Reference:** Link to the inspiration, if available.
- **Status:** Proposed, approved, in production, or published.
```

Credit external sources and make sure the team has permission to publish any media it uploads.
